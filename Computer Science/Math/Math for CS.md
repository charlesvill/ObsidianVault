Calculus pre work: 
### 1. Prerequisite Topic Checklist

(Check off as you gain confidence)

- **Algebra & Functions**
    
    - Exponents: integer, fractional, negative
        
    - Polynomial operations: expand, factor
        
    - Rational expressions (simplify, divide, multiply)
        
    - Linear, quadratic, higher-degree equation solving
        
    - Exponential / logarithmic functions: rules/laws, inverses
        
    - Function basics: domain, range, composition, inverse
        
- **Trigonometry**
    
    - Right triangles: sine, cosine, tangent (SOH-CAH-TOA)
        
    - Unit circle, degrees ↔ radians conversion
        
    - Graphs of trig functions: period, amplitude, shifts
        
    - Basic identities: Pythagorean; sum/difference; double/half-angle
        
    - Solving trig equations
        
    - Law of sines & law of cosines
        
    - Inverse trig functions
        
- **Pre-Calculus / Geometry Essentials**
    
    - Coordinate geometry (distance formula, midpoint, line equations)
        
    - Conic sections basics (circle, parabola)
        
    - Simple limits intuition (behavior as x→a, x→∞)
        
    - Sequences & series: arithmetic & geometric
        
    - Sigma (∑) notation, basic sums
